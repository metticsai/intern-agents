## Instagram / Meta — fields per variant:
- headline: Bold text shown BELOW the image. Under 40 chars. Action-oriented. (e.g. "Brambleton's #1 Plumber")
- hook: Primary text shown ABOVE the image. Under 125 chars. This is the first thing they read — make it stop the scroll.
- body: 2-3 sentences that build on the hook. Specific, emotional, local. Use 2-4 emojis naturally.
- cta: MUST be exactly one of these Meta button options (nothing else): Book Now / Call Now / Get Quote / Learn More / Contact Us / Sign Up / Shop Now / Send Message
  Pick the CTA that fits the business type: restaurants/cafes/salons/gyms → Book Now or Learn More; contractors/trades/repairs → Get Quote or Call Now; retail/boutiques → Shop Now. NEVER use Get Quote for a restaurant, cafe, salon, or store.
- hashtags: 6-8 tags mixing local (#BrambletonVA), niche (#LocalPlumber), and broad (#HomeRepair). String format.
- image_prompt: Vertical 4:5 portrait for Instagram feed. [See IMAGE PROMPT RULES below]

## TikTok — fields per variant:
- overlay: Bold text shown ON the image. Under 34 chars. Punchy, casual, Gen-Z tone. (e.g. "POV: Your drain is fixed in 1 hour")
- caption: Short caption below the post. Under 150 chars. Written like a real person talking, not a brand. No corporate language.
- cta: Short and conversational — max 30 chars (e.g. "Link in bio 👇" / "Comment below" / "Follow for more")
- hashtags: 6-8 tags including trending formats (#POV, #SmallBusiness, #LocalFinds) plus niche and location. String format.
- image_prompt: Vertical 9:16 portrait. [See IMAGE PROMPT RULES below] UGC-style, raw, authentic.

## LinkedIn — fields per variant:
- hook: Opening line visible before "...see more". Professional, credibility-first. Under 150 chars. No fluff.
- body: 3-4 credibility-building sentences. Up to 1300 chars total including hook. Can use short bullet points (•) for services or stats.
- cta: Professional (Learn more / Let's connect / Visit our website / Message us)
- hashtags: 4-6 professional hashtags (#SmallBusiness #LocalBusiness + industry-specific). String format.
- image_prompt: Horizontal 16:9 landscape. [See IMAGE PROMPT RULES below] Clean, professional.

## IMAGE PROMPT RULES

image_prompt must describe a cinematic, photorealistic scene that could run in a premium Instagram campaign.
DO NOT include any text, logos, words, or typography in the image — text is added separately as an overlay.
Use the BUSINESS TYPE and IMAGE STYLE from the context to guide the aesthetic.

Structure every image_prompt as: [Shot type] of [specific subject] in [specific setting], [lighting], [color grade], [mood/atmosphere], shot on Sony A7R IV, f/1.8 bokeh background, photorealistic, Instagram editorial photography, ultra-high detail, 4:5 vertical crop. IMPORTANT: compose the shot so the main subject fills the upper 60% of the frame — the lower 40% should be a clean, simple background (blurred, out of focus, or minimal) as it will have ad copy placed over it.

PRODUCT RULE (applies to every image_prompt): if BRAND SIGNALS or headings name a specific product, dish, or service, that exact thing must be the visual subject of at least 2 of the 3 variants. Never default to generic people in generic settings when a real product exists.

LIGHTING RULE (applies to every image_prompt): default to BRIGHT, well-lit, vibrant scenes — natural daylight, airy, colorful, inviting. Always include words like "bright natural daylight" and "vibrant colors" in the prompt. NEVER describe dark, moody, dim, candlelit, or low-key scenes. Ads must feel fresh and energetic, not atmospheric.

Style guide by business type:
- home_services: Close-up or medium shot of technician actively working — wrench in hand, focused expression, bright natural daylight streaming through a window. Real residential setting, not a showroom. Warm clean tones, shallow depth of field.
- food_beverage: Hero shot of the signature dish or drink, steam rising, vibrant saturated colors that make the food look irresistible, bright natural daylight from a large window, fresh ingredients visible, clean bright background — think fresh daytime food photography, appetizing and colorful, never dark or candlelit.
- health_beauty: Over-the-shoulder medium shot of client mid-service, soft bright window light, clean minimal background, serene expression, fresh glowing skin tones, light and airy.
- health_fitness: Dynamic shot of person mid-movement — weights, running, yoga — bright energetic lighting, vivid colors, sunlit gym or outdoor golden morning light, powerful and aspirational.
- retail: HERO SHOT OF THE ACTUAL PRODUCT — the specific product named in the brand signals, shown close-up and in sharp detail (on a desk, in hand, mid-use), bright natural daylight, clean background. The product is the star; people appear only as hands or partial context, never as the subject.
- professional_services: Two people in genuine eye contact at a modern desk or coffee meeting, bright airy office, confident and warm, natural highlights, crisp and clean.
- general_business: Candid medium shot of real people in the actual business environment, bright natural light, genuine moment of interaction or work, editorial feel.
